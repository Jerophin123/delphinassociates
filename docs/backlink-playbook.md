---

## What NOT to do

- Never buy backlinks, use PBNs, or mass directory-submission services —
  Google and Bing both penalize these, and recovery is slow.
- Don't stuff exact-match anchors ("construction company Chennai") everywhere;
  branded anchors should dominate.

## Tracking

- Bing: Webmaster Tools → Backlinks (the report that raised this flag).
- Google: Search Console → Links.
- Recheck the Bing "inbound links" recommendation ~4-6 weeks after completing Tier 1 + 2.
